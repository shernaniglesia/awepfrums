Key Features

Real-Time Room Tracking

Monitor 6 classrooms & 6 computer labs. Instructors can declare room availability during cancellations for optimal utilization.

Android & Web Access

Access the system seamlessly from smartphones, tablets, or desktops. AWE-based design ensures accessibility everywhere.

Equipment Inventory

Track projectors, extension cords, connectors & more. Automated borrowing logs with borrower details and return tracking.

Multi-User Access

Admin, Instructors, and Students with role-based permissions for booking, monitoring, and inventory management.
